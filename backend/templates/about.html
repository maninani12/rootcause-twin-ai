HOW IT WORKS

Experience that stays with the factory.

Persistent factory intelligence that learns from every investigation.

The problem

Factories often separate maintenance incidents, quality defects and troubleshooting outcomes. Teams repeat failed fixes because the full operating context is hard to retrieve.

RootCause Twin

Connects operational context, failed fixes, verified root causes and outcomes. Hindsight provides persistent organizational experience across investigations.

Without Hindsight, RootCause Twin is a generic troubleshooting assistant. With Hindsight, it learns from verified organizational experience including failed and successful corrective actions.

The memory loop

  1. RETAIN

    Stores human-verified incident outcomes, including failed actions.

  2. RECALL

    Retrieves relevant previous troubleshooting experiences.

  3. REFLECT · FUTURE

    Optional pattern reasoning; this MVP uses deterministic context comparison and does not call Reflect.
